# Reports & Visualizations Directory

This directory contains analytical reports and generated figure charts from the EMR Adoption & Performance study.

## Visualization Categories

All plots are auto-generated by running `notebooks/03_final_analysis.ipynb` and are stored under `reports/figures/`:

### 1. Demographics (`reports/figures/demographics/`)
Visualizations exploring healthcare worker distributions:
- `age.png`: Age distribution of respondents.
- `gender.png`: Gender composition across facilities.
- `education_level.png`: Highest qualification levels of respondents.
- `profession_distiution.png`: Role breakdown across professions (nurses, doctors, lab personnel, IT, admin).

### 2. EMR Technical Complexity & User Perception (`reports/figures/technical_complexity/`)
Charts assessing EMR usability and correlation analysis:
- `emr_complexity_perception.png` & `EMR_complexity.png`: Perceived system complexity.
- `profession_.emr_complexity.png`: Perceived difficulty segmented by healthcare profession.
- `EMR_complexity_rating_grid.png` & `emr_complexity_rating_big.png`: Rating distributions grid.
- `timely_completion_of_emr.png` & `Timelytask_completion.png`: Efficiency impact on workflow timing.
- `self_rated_confidence.png`: User confidence levels in operating EMR modules.

### 3. Infrastructure & Organizational Support (`reports/figures/infrastructure_support/`)
Assessment of facility readiness and operational challenges:
- `network_connectivity_challenge_grid.png` & `network_connectivity_per department.png`: Internet & network outage severity across departments.
- `Alternative Power solution.png`: Availability of backup power systems during power cuts.
- `technical_support time by population.png` & `technical_support vs training in the last 12 months.png`: IT support turnaround times & training coverage.
- `leadership_perception.png` & `user_confidence by supervisory monitoring.png`: Impact of supervisory oversight and management support.
- `healthinfopolicy.png` & `healthinformationpolicybydepartment.png`: Adherence to health information governance policies.
- `EMR Adoption Success by Action Plan Presence .png`: EMR implementation success rates tied to facility action plans.
